What happens when someone opens the site

  1. STEP 1

    Visitor opens a page

    Someone lands on egbgroups.in from a Facebook ad, Google or a direct link.

  2. STEP 2

    Site loads the pixel

    The code we added in layout.tsx downloads fbevents.js from Facebook.

  3. STEP 3

    Pixel identifies itself

    fbq('init', '1756351712281814') tells Meta which account this site belongs to.

  4. STEP 4

    PageView is sent

    fbq('track', 'PageView') sends a small request to facebook.com/tr.

  5. STEP 5

    Meta records it

    The visit appears in Events Manager. The SEO team can use it for ads and retargeting.

Visitor → EGB site → fbevents.js → facebook.com/tr → Meta Events Manager → Ads

Common questions

Is the pixel working?

Yes. "Active" with ID 1756351712281814 matches the ID your SEO team sent.

Do customers see this panel?

No. It exists only in your browser because you installed the extension.

What does it track right now?

Page views only. Enquiry clicks, calls and form submissions need extra events added later.

What should we do next?

Ask the SEO team to open Meta Events Manager and confirm PageView events are arriving.

Meta may ask for a cookie consent banner depending on where your visitors are. Check with whoever handles your privacy policy.
